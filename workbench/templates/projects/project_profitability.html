{% translate 'Assumptions' %}

{% blocktranslate trimmed %} All figures are estimates. They should rather underestimate than overestimate the profitability. When in doubt, hours are counted and money is left out. {% endblocktranslate %}

  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %} The gross margin is the largest of the accepted offers' total, the projected invoices and the invoiced total. Closed projects only use the invoiced total. Third party costs are always deducted: the larger of the third party costs on invoices and the logged third party costs, so that logged costs which haven't been invoiced yet are deducted too. {% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %} Budget transferred to other projects is deducted, budget transferred from other projects is added. {% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %} Service hours are the hours entered in the services, except for optional services and services of declined offers or budget retainers. Hours of offers which haven't been accepted yet are included even though their amount isn't. {% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %} The relevant hours are the service hours or the hours logged on these services, whichever is larger. Hours logged on other services (for example optional services) are always added on top. {% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% blocktranslate trimmed %} The rate for accepted offers only uses their amount and their hours. It shows how profitable the project is if no further offers are accepted and no work happens outside of the accepted offers. {% endblocktranslate %}
