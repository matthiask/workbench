# Deals

Deals are the sales pipeline: what might become an order, how likely and
when it's decided. They need access to deals.

## Creating a deal

"Deals" → "+ Deal", or "New" → "Deal" on an organization or a person.

- **Customer** and **contact**; **contact person** is who's responsible
  for the deal, you by default.
- **Probability**: low, normal or high. High needs the expected
  decision date.
- **Decision expected on**, with shortcuts for one week to three months.
- **Value**: an amount per value type (e.g. per kind of work); the
  deal's value is their sum.
- **Driving force**: who contributes, normally or as main driving force.
  The contact person always contributes. The accepted deals report
  splits won values by these contributions.

## The pipeline

The deals list shows open deals grouped by probability and expected
decision: high in the next 4 weeks (overdue ones included), in the next
12 weeks, later, then normal and low probability, each with its sum.
Filter by contributor to see your deals. A deal's badge turns orange
when its decision is overdue or when it has been open for long. The start
page lists open deals you contribute to.

## Offers

"Link offer" links offers in preparation or offered to an open deal; an
offer of another customer's project needs a confirmation (e.g. an
agency and its client). The project shows the deals next to its offers.

Offers can also be linked to an accepted deal later, unless they have
been declined, e.g. the detailed offer of a contract the deal sums up;
nothing changes, the offer keeps its status. The accepted deals report
counts accepted offers without deal on their own, so a missing link
counts the work twice.

"Link project" links projects to open or accepted deals, for work sold
without offer such as a maintenance contract or a mandate. The accepted
deals report counts the invoices of maintenance projects without deal
on their own; a linked project is covered by its deal.

## Closing a deal

The **Status** button closes the deal as **accepted** (with the award of
contract) or **declined** (with the reason for losing), on a date and
with an optional notice. The linked offers are accepted or declined
with it; untick those which should stay as they are. When declining,
Workbench asks whether to delete the planned work of the offers after
the current week.

Reopening a deal clears its closing date, type and notice, but leaves the
offers as they are. Offers can only be unlinked from open deals.
