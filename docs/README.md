# Help

Workbench records the work of the agency: contacts, deals, projects with
their offers, services, logged hours and costs, invoices, planning,
absences and the reports built from them. This help explains how to use
Workbench. How your company works with it (naming, rates, who plans
whom) is in its handbook.

- [Logging hours](hours.md): after each task, with timestamps when
  that isn't possible, breaks
- [Offers](offers.md): services, prices, statuses, copies and PDFs
- [Invoices](invoices.md): the types, services invoices, down payments,
  sending, payments and reminders
- [Keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md): jumping to projects and people,
  logging hours, costs and breaks

## What Workbench doesn't do

- **Tasks and issues** stay in the task management tool. Projects and
  milestones in Workbench are about budgets, hours and dates, not about
  the individual tasks.
- **Files** such as designs, contracts and correspondence aren't stored
  in Workbench, apart from the PDFs of offers and invoices it creates.
