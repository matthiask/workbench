# Projects

Everything for a customer happens in a project: its services and logged
hours and costs, offers, invoices and planning.

## Creating a project

"Projects" → "+ Project", or "New" → "Project" on an organization or a
person, which fills in the customer. "Copy" in a project's menu starts a
new project with its details. Projects are numbered per year
(e.g. 2026-0042).

| Type | |
| --- | --- |
| Order | Work for a customer with a budget, usually from offers. |
| Maintenance | Normally fully chargeable, no cost ceiling. |
| Internal | Work for the company itself; where enabled with an internal type (e.g. outreach). |

The project description describes the project for the team; offers have
their own descriptions. Depending on your access, a project also has a
**campaign** (grouping projects of a customer), a cost center, a flat
rate for all its services and a [budget alert](reports.md#projects).

## Services

Hours and costs are always logged on a service. Create services with
"+ Service" on the project, in the [offer form](offers.md) or while
[logging hours](hours.md). A service's menu reassigns its logbook
entries to other services (except entries on an invoice), merges it into another service or moves it to another project
(only services without offer and not invoiced). Services which have
been invoiced can't be deleted.

## Closing a project

Set "closed on" in the project's form ("today" fills it in). Closing:

- discards offers in preparation and declines those sent before,
- asks whether to delete the planned work after the current week,
- keeps logging possible for 14 days, with a warning, then locks it.

Clear the date to reopen the project. The project list shows open
projects by default; "Old projects" lists those without activity for 60
days, which the start page also suggests closing.
