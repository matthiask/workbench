# Absences and working time

## Absences

"Logbook" → "Absences" lists everybody's absences, "+ Absence" adds one.
An absence has a start, an optional end (else it's a single day), the
number of **days**, the reason and a description.

| Reason | |
| --- | --- |
| Vacation | Counts as working time, up to your vacation entitlement. |
| Sickness | Counts as working time. Only you and personnel administration see the description. |
| Paid leave | E.g. civil service, parental leave or moving house. Counts as working time, confirmed by personnel administration. |
| School attendance of apprentices | Counts as working time. |
| Other reasons (no working time) | Shown in the calendar and in planning, but doesn't count as working time. |
| Working time correction | Entered by personnel administration only. |

**Days** are working days at full time, also when you work part-time:
someone working 80% who is off for a week enters 4 days, not 5. Below
the field, the suggestion counts the weekdays without holidays times
your percentage; check it when you don't work the same hours every day.
"Enter hours" converts hours into days.

- An absence starts and ends in the same year. Split a vacation over New
  Year into two absences.
- Absences in past years can only be created and changed where late
  logging is allowed.
- You can enter absences for others, e.g. when someone calls in sick.
  Changing and deleting them is up to the absent person, personnel
  administration and, on the same day, whoever entered them.
- Paid leave counts right away but stays "Unconfirmed" until personnel
  administration confirms it; changes by others reset the confirmation.
- A sickness absence with a description can't be changed to another
  reason, since the description would become visible to everybody.
  Replace it with a new absence without description instead (the error
  links to that).

The **absence calendar** ("Reports" → "Absence calendar", also on teams)
shows everybody's absences on a timeline, for the next twelve months or
a year, filtered by team. Clicking an absence opens it.

## Annual working time

"Reports" → "Annual working time" shows your year, month by month:

- **Target time**: the working days of the month without holidays,
  times your percentage and the hours per day.
- **Working time**: the hours you logged on any project plus your
  absences (days times the hours per day). Breaks don't count as working
  time.
- **Balance per month**: working time minus target time; the **running
  balance** adds them up from January.
- **Vacation days**: your entitlement, the days taken and planned and
  those left. Vacation beyond the entitlement doesn't count as working
  time. Workbench reminds you to plan your vacation when less than half
  of it is planned.

The **balance** tile shows the running balance at the end of the last
completed month. The weekly chart counts up to today, the year-end
balance includes all twelve months and the vacation days left, so the
three may differ. Future months already count their full target time,
so the totals of the whole year are negative during the year.

"Active users" lists everybody's vacation days; other people's details
and sickness days are only visible to personnel administration. Working
time models, employments and holidays are set up by the administrators.

## The rings at the top

The two rings next to your name show today's and this week's logged
hours against the targets: a full working day, and five days times your
percentage. Absences and holidays don't lower the targets. A click
lists your logged hours.
